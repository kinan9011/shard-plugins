# Changelog

## 1.1.0

- Follows the app language: English, German, Arabic, French, Spanish, Chinese, Japanese and Hebrew, with right-to-left layout for Arabic and Hebrew. Any other language shows English.

## 1.0.1

- Restyled after the JetBrains New UI to match Shard's new look: flat surfaces, 4px corners, compact controls and the Int UI colors and fonts sent by the app.

## 1.0.0

Moved out of Shard. The hashes are computed by a WebAssembly module.
